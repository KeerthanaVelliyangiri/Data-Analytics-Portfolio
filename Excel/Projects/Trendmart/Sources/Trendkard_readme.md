# 👗 TrendMart Fashion Enterprise 

## 📌 Project Overview

TrendMart Fashion Enterprise is an **Excel-based Data Analytics project** developed to analyze sales, cost, profit, discounts, returns, categories, sales channels, and city performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

TrendMart generates sales across multiple products, stores, and sales channels, but management lacks a consolidated view of profitability.

The project aims to identify:

- Areas with high sales but comparatively low profitability
- Discount and profitability patterns
- Return patterns
- Category performance
- Online vs Offline performance
- City and regional performance

### Core Business Question

> **Where is TrendMart losing potential profitability despite generating sales?**

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze Sales, Cost, Profit, and Profit Margin
- Understand discount and profitability patterns
- Analyze return activity
- Compare category performance
- Compare Online and Offline channels
- Compare city and regional performance
- Identify areas requiring management attention

# 🔍 Key Insights

### 📉 1. Sales & Profit Trend

Sales and profit show significant variation across the analyzed months, with a noticeable decline during parts of 2025.

### 💸 2. Discount Performance

Higher discount levels show lower observed profit contribution in the analyzed data.

### 🔄 3. Return Analysis

146 transaction records were marked as **Returned**, while 2,854 were marked as **Not Returned**.

### 👗 4. Category Performance

Category performance varies across **Sales, Cost, and Profit**, showing the importance of evaluating profitability rather than sales alone.

### 🛒 5. Online vs Offline Performance

Offline sales contribute more than Online sales in the analyzed dataset.

- **Offline Sales:** ₹60.49 L
- **Online Sales:** ₹31.78 L

### 🌆 6. City Performance

Sales and profit vary across cities, allowing management to identify locations requiring further investigation.

---

# 💡 Recommendations

- Monitor monthly sales and profit changes
- Review high-discount segments for profitability
- Track returns by category, channel, and location
- Evaluate categories using Sales, Cost, and Profit together
- Compare Online and Offline profitability
- Investigate weaker-performing cities and business segments
- Monitor potential profitability leakage across products, categories, stores, and channels

---

# 🎯 Conclusion

The TrendMart dashboard provides a consolidated view of **sales, cost, profit, discounts, returns, categories, channels, and city performance**.

It helps management identify important business patterns and areas requiring further investigation to understand potential profitability leakage.
